Press Certify to search the product of the cart tapes and the resource monitor for the shortest intervention plan.
THE FORMAL OBJECT. Each cart is a finite automaton. The board is their synchronous product with a resource monitor. A deadlock is a product state with no progress transition; a livelock is a cycle with no acceptance. The certifier searches that graph, not a script.
THE WINDOW. A cycle can only be broken while it is still open. A hold placed after the last edge closes has no continuation; the ledger says so instead of pretending the board is unwinnable.
WHY THE LOAN IS NOT A SPEED-UP. A loan attaches to the holder, not the waiter, and it expires. Boosting the blocked cart is a no-op. That is priority inheritance, and it is the whole inversion lesson.

THE FORMAL OBJECT. A depth-two oral-message tree. Each traitor chooses a value for each outgoing link; honest towers relay what they heard. The decision is a majority at the leaf. The certifier enumerates the tree and returns the worst witness.
WHY DELAY IS NOT A CUT. A late round-one order still counts in the recipient's own decision but cannot be relayed. A late round-two relay misses the decision entirely. Delay is an interrogation; cut is a quarantine.
THE THRESHOLD IS THE GAME. n=4, f=1 is exact; n=5 has one tower of slack; n=3 cannot guarantee agreement with oral messages. The tribunal shows which one you are playing.

Tri-colour invariant

White is unscanned. Grey is discovered and waiting. Black is scanned. During a mark, no black block may point to a white block. A store that creates one is a dirty edge; the sweep would lose the target. The traces here are rooted DAGs: reachability, not allocation order, decides what is live.

THE VERB. Shade the edge, sweep the dead, slide the live. The pointer rewrite is automatic; the cost and the seam are yours.

Pause is a deadline

Every operation is charged in cells. A full slide of a large live set can cost more than the whole pause; a mark can be split across pauses. Free cells are not usable cells: allocation needs a contiguous run.

CERTIFIER. The product of the mutator trace and the heap. A schedule is a path through that product, not a guess. Peak pause is the score that matters.

Read the scope

The waveform is the program: ALLOC, STORE, ROOT, RETIRE. The next four events are visible. The cursor is the present. A red ALLOC WAIT means the heap is fragmented, not merely full.
